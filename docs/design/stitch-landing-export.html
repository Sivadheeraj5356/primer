Chapter II

Watch it adapt

A calm, respectful companion that learns how your child learns. No stress, no screens demanding streaks, and no overwhelming dashboards.

Beat 01

The book notices what your child practices

Rather than testing with anxiety-inducing timers, Primer observes how comfortably your child pauses, which phoneme sounds they repeat, and which stories capture their curiosity naturally.

Observation without judgment
Beat 02

It gently strengthens what is weak

When a letter shape like "g" or a blend like "wh" causes hesitation, Barnaby introduces a fresh tale where that exact sound takes center stage in the woodland adventure, weaving remedy directly into story.

Warm reinforcement
Beat 03

It brings old skills back before they fade

Based on a quiet spaced repetition rhythm, familiar words and number pairs quietly resurface in new chapters after five days, locking foundational confidence in place without flashcard drills.

Enduring mastery
